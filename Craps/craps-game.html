🎲 How to Play Craps

Basic Rules

Craps is played with two dice. Players bet on the outcome of dice rolls.

Come-Out Roll (First Roll)

  • 7 or 11 (Natural): Pass Line bets win (pay 1:1). Don't Pass loses.
  • 2 or 3 (Craps): Pass Line bets lose. Don't Pass wins.
  • 12 (Boxcars): Pass Line loses. Don't Pass pushes (bet returned). This is still called craps.
  • 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, or 10: Point is established - continue rolling

Point Phase

Once a point is established, you must roll that number again before rolling a 7.

  • Roll the Point: Pass Line bets win (pay 1:1)
  • Roll 7 (Seven Out): Pass Line bets lose, Don't Pass bets win
  • Other Numbers: Continue rolling (no resolution)

Available Bets

  • Pass Line: Win on 7/11 (come-out) or the point (point phase)
  • Don't Pass Bar: Win on 2/3 (come-out) or 7 (point phase). 12 is a push.
  • Come: Same rules as Pass Line
  • Field: Win on 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11, 12 (2 and 12 pay 2:1)
  • 3 or 11 (15 to 1): One-roll bet. Wins on 3 or 11 only
  • 2 or 12 (30 to 1): One-roll bet. Wins on 2 or 12 only
  • Any Craps (7 to 1): One-roll bet. Wins on 2, 3, or 12
  • Big 6 & 8: Win on 6 or 8 (pays 1:1)

Strategy Tips

  • Pass Line has the lowest house edge (1.41%)
  • Don't Pass has even lower house edge (1.36%)
  • Avoid proposition bets (high house edge)
  • Manage your bankroll wisely
